# GDPR & IP-tracking content gap: Albacross vs AI-cited competitors

**Run date:** 2026-11-02 · **Author:** ai-seo-content-strategist (agent) · **Status:** draft. Legal must review before any page goes live.
**Why this matters:** the five competitor pages below earn 50 AI citations in 30 days (Peec, 2026-10-04 → 2026-11-02). Albacross has no page that AI engines cite on these questions. The target prompts are P18 "Is website visitor identification legal under GDPR?", P38 "Is Albacross GDPR compliant?", P39 "How accurate is Albacross company identification?" and P03 (GDPR-compliant tools for Europe).

**Competitor pages studied** (all checked 2026-11-02; text was not copied):
- **LI-GDPR**: [Leadinfo, 5 FAQs on IP tracking & GDPR](https://www.leadinfo.com/en/blog/the-5-most-frequently-asked-questions-about-ip-tracking-tools-and-gdpr). 16 citations. Published 2025-12-02, updated 2026-07-24. About 1,300 words.
- **FA**: [Factors, website deanonymization](https://www.factors.ai/blog/identify-anonymous-website-visitor). 13 citations. 2026-10-19, named author. About 3,200 words.
- **LF-WV**: [Leadfeeder /web-visitors](https://www.leadfeeder.com/web-visitors/). 11 citations. Product page with a 10-question FAQ. About 2,000 words.
- **LI-REL**: [Leadinfo, how reliable is IP tracking](https://www.leadinfo.com/en/blog/how-reliable-is-ip-tracking-for-identifying-website-visitors). 5 citations. Dated 2025-11. About 1,400 words.
- **LF-GDPR**: [Leadfeeder, GDPR website tracking](https://www.leadfeeder.com/blog/website-visitor-identification/website-visitor-tracking-in-a-post-gdpr-world-what-b2bs-need-to-know). 5 citations. 2026-10-13, named author. About 1,850 words.

**Why these pages get cited:**
1. **The headings are the buyer's question,** and the first sentence under each one answers it ("Yes, under legitimate interest, if…").
2. **They give a named legal hook,** usually Art. 6(1)(f), the ePrivacy vs GDPR split, Schrems II or the fine ceiling.
3. **They publish their own numbers:** LF-WV "up to 45%", LI-REL "35–40%", FA "up to 75%", a database of 220M profiles, data centres in Ireland and Frankfurt, ISO 27001, opt-out handled within 48 h.
4. **They define terms:** coverage vs accuracy (FA), company-level vs person-level (FA, LF), controller vs processor (LF-GDPR).
5. **They are dated in 2025–2026,** and two carry a named author.
6. **They end with an FAQ block** of 4–10 Qs.

**What the competitors miss:**
- None cites the EDPB, a CJEU case or a recital. LF-GDPR comes closest: it explains the ePrivacy/GDPR split and admits "this involves personal data". Leadinfo says "no personal data", which is legally weak.
- **None mentions EDPB Guidelines 2/2023 §3.3, "Tracking based on IP only"** (v2 adopted 2024-10-16). It says Art. 5(3) ePrivacy *can* apply to IP-only tracking (paras 54–56). That one fact is the most citable gap in the market.

**Contradictions in Albacross's own published material** (Legal must resolve these before the hub is written):
- **C1. Legal basis.** The help FAQ says consent is required for the script. The newsroom says the basis is "usually legitimate interest". The privacy policy (2024-10-02) uses legitimate interest for Albacross's own processing. `facts.yaml → legal_basis` is still VERIFY. The cookie lifetime also conflicts: 1 year in the checklist vs 24 months in the privacy policy.
- **Line-by-line fixes** for /newsroom/albacross-gdpr and the ebook GDPR chapter are in `ai-seo-agent/research/gdpr-assets-refresh-audit.md`.
- **C2. Cookies.** The checklist names two 1-year cookies, and /identify promotes first-party cookies. Sales says there are "cookieless / hybrid / consent-gated" modes (VOC), but no page says so.
- **C3. Fingerprinting.** The help FAQ lists a "fingerprint hash" among the data processed. Competitors present "no fingerprinting" as a compliance point.
- **C4. Hosting.** The privacy policy says "servers located in Ireland", and the DPA lists AWS Ireland. But no marketing page states this, and the DPA safeguards column is blank for the UK, US and "Global" sub-processors.
- **C5. Company data.** The 2020 post says company data is not personal data. That conflicts with the DPA, whose data subjects include employees of the companies identified.
- **C6. Script timing.** A VOC rep note records advising a prospect to load the script before consent. Legal must review this.
- **C7. Contact consent.** The help FAQ says contacts "consented" via the data partner. Legal needs to confirm this.
