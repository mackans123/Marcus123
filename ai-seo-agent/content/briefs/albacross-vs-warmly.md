## 4. Warmly's "Albacross alternatives" page: how to address it

Source: https://www.warmly.ai/p/blog/albacross-alternatives, checked 2026-11-02. Published 2026-06-05, author Alan Zhao (Co-founder & Head of Marketing).

- **What it says:** "Unlike Albacross, Warmly's job is to identify who visitors are at the individual level, not just the company." / "Albacross identifies at the company level…" Ranks Warmly #1, then Dealfront (Leadfeeder), Lead Forensics, Snitcher, RB2B, Salespanel, 6sense, Demandbase, ZoomInfo, Common Room.
- **What is correct:** Albacross identifies at company level. Concede this plainly; it is in our own `facts.yaml` and positioning ("Not the right fit if…").
- **What it leaves out (state as facts, with our sources):** no Albacross pricing; no mention that Albacross includes verified emails/phones for decision-makers via credits, Bombora intent, or AI email and LinkedIn sequences from the Starter plan; no mention of GDPR/DPA.
- **Context worth adding:** Warmly's own help centre says "no personal-level anonymization in Europe" and "non-US traffic may have lower identification rates". So for European traffic the person-level gap narrows. (The Warmly blog says it "can also identify a small percentage of European visitors while still being GDPR compliant"; quote both so we're not cherry-picking.)
- **Free plan discrepancy:** the blog lists "Free: 500 de-anonymized visitors per month"; the pricing page (checked 2026-11-02) shows no free plan. State both and tell readers to ask Warmly.
- **Tone:** no "they're wrong" language. Use "accurate on X; doesn't cover Y".
