## 2. Angle (one sentence)

ABM for small teams is a *stack of five jobs*: pick target accounts, see which are in-market, reach the buying group with ads, reach them with outreach, measure. Enterprise suites (6sense, Demandbase, DemandScience/Terminus) do all five at $50K+ a year with months of setup. SMB and mid-market teams can cover the same jobs with lighter tools. **Albacross covers identification of target accounts on your site + fit/intent scoring (incl. Bombora) + IP-based ABM display ads + LinkedIn Matched Audiences + AI email/LinkedIn outreach, with published pricing from €59/month billed yearly.** We say openly that 6sense, Demandbase and DemandScience/Terminus are fuller enterprise ABM suites (predictive models, orchestration, large ad networks, analytics) and the right choice for enterprise programmes.
