Also cover in short H3s or a callout:
- **Account-level vs contact-level intent** (lemlist, Autobound both have it). Bombora/6sense are account-level; RB2B/Warmly reveal people (US only); Albacross is company-level plus contacts via credits.
- **Bidstream data** (one paragraph: scale, lower accuracy, privacy risk). Shows expertise.
- **"Intent data vs buying signals"** (whitewhale's and autobound's top FAQ). Honest answer: signals are a broader set; intent is research behaviour.
- **How Bombora Company Surge works**: 3-week activity vs 12-week baseline per account-topic pair; consent-driven tag on co-op sites; 25,300+ topics; 86% of co-op data exclusive. <!-- src: https://bombora.com/intent/, checked 2026-11-02 -->

- **Activation**: what happens after the signal (CRM sync, Slack/Teams alert, LinkedIn audience, ABM ads, sequence). Link /automate, /engage, /linkedin.
- **Budget bands**: <$5K/yr (website intent tools, Apollo), $5–25K (Factors, Warmly, G2 Core, Cognism entry), $25–150K+ (Bombora direct, 6sense, Demandbase, ZoomInfo full intent, TechTarget). Source each band in comments.
