**TL;DR box (50–70 words, wording approved by Legal).** Direction:
- It can be lawful under GDPR, but it is personal-data processing, because IP addresses are personal data.
- Company-level identification is lower risk than identifying individuals.
- You need a documented legal basis (usually legitimate interest, assessed case by case), a privacy notice with an objection route, a DPA with the vendor, and a separate ePrivacy (cookie/terminal access) check of how the script runs.

**"Key facts" box (dated, citable):**

2. **H2 Is an IP address personal data under GDPR?** Cover Art. 4(1), Recital 30 (online identifiers) and *Breyer*: a dynamic IP is personal data where the site operator has legal means to identify the person. Mention *IAB Europe* (C-604/22, 2024-03-07) on combinable identifiers only if Legal wants it. Albacross's own DPA lists IPv4/IPv6 as personal data, so say so plainly.
3. **H2 Is company data personal data?** Cover Recital 14: data about legal persons is outside GDPR. 5. **H2 GDPR vs ePrivacy: do you need cookie consent?**
   - **H3 The two rulebooks.** GDPR asks whether you have a legal basis to process personal data. ePrivacy Art. 5(3) asks whether you may store or read information on the visitor's device. They are separate tests (cite *Planet49*, C-673/17, 2019-10-01: Art. 5(3) applies whether or not the data is personal, and consent requires an active act).
   - **H3 Does "cookieless" IP-only tracking avoid consent?** **This is the gap section.** EDPB Guidelines 2/2023 §3.3, paras 54–56, say:
     - Art. 5(3) *can* apply to tracking based only on the IP address, where the IP originates from the user's terminal (e.g. a static IPv4 from the user's router, or IPv6).
     - It is not systematic (e.g. CGNAT).
     - Applicability doesn't automatically mean consent is needed, because exemptions must be assessed.
     - Close with: so "no cookies" ≠ "no ePrivacy analysis". `[[LEGAL: approve the wording and conclusion]]`
   - **H3 UK and national rules.** Cover UK GDPR + PECR, the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 changes to storage/access exemptions `[[VERIFY: commencement date and scope]]`, and Germany's TDDDG (formerly TTDSG) `[[VERIFY]]`. One sentence each; no deep dive.
