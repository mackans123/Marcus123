## Person-level vs company-level identification

**Warmly tries to name the individual visitor; Albacross identifies the company behind the visit and then gives you verified contacts for the right decision-makers there.** That is the core difference, and it decides most evaluations.

Warmly's own numbers on how much traffic it identifies are worth reading closely, because two Warmly pages give different figures:

- Warmly's help centre: "it typically only identifies up to ~5% of traffic at the individual-level", with a "Company identification rate around 60%" and a "Contact identification rate around 45%". <!-- src: https://help.warmly.ai/articles/5643444357-overview-of-warmly-s-data-data-quality, checked 2026-11-02 -->
- Warmly's June 2026 blog: "around 15% of visitors at the person level and roughly 65% at the company level" on typical B2B traffic. <!-- src: https://www.warmly.ai/p/blog/albacross-alternatives, checked 2026-11-02 -->

Either way, most identified traffic is at company level for Warmly too. Person-level matches are a valuable minority, and they are mostly US visitors.

Albacross does not name anonymous individual visitors. If your SDRs need the named person who read your pricing page, and that person is in the US, Warmly (or RB2B, below) is the better choice. If your team is happy to see the company, its fit and intent, and then reach the buying committee with verified emails and phones, Albacross covers that at a lower price.

## Europe and GDPR

**If most of your traffic is European, Warmly's person-level advantage mostly falls away. Its help centre says it does "no personal-level anonymization in Europe".** <!-- src: https://help.warmly.ai/articles/5643444357-overview-of-warmly-s-data-data-quality, checked 2026-11-02 --> The Warmly blog adds that Warmly "can also identify a small percentage of European visitors while still being GDPR compliant". <!-- src: https://www.warmly.ai/p/blog/albacross-alternatives, checked 2026-11-02 -->

Albacross identifies companies, not people, and is built for GDPR and CCPA compliance with a [Data Processing Agreement](/data-processing-agreement) available. That makes the legal review simpler: your team is processing information about organisations visiting your site, not profiling named individuals. Our [GDPR page](/newsroom/albacross-gdpr) sets out how it works. [[VERIFY with legal: legitimate-interest wording and EU hosting statement before adding them here]]

Whichever tool you choose, ask for the vendor's DPA, sub-processor list and legal basis under GDPR Article 6, and have your own counsel confirm it. ([GDPR text, EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj)) European buyers we talk to often say their legal team wants written answers before the script goes live, so collect them during the trial, not after.

## Intent data and outreach

**Both tools use Bombora for off-site research intent, so intent data alone won't separate them.** Warmly says "Warmly partners with Bombora to provide intent data" and that Bombora "may be included or available as an add-on" depending on plan; its GTM Signals Package costs $10,000 a year extra. <!-- src: https://www.warmly.ai/p/signals/third-party and https://www.warmly.ai/pricing, checked 2026-11-02 --> Albacross combines on-site behaviour with Bombora research intent to score accounts by fit and intent. [[VERIFY: which Albacross plans include Bombora intent]]

The bigger difference is how each tool acts on the signal:

- **Warmly** acts on the website itself and through agents: AI chat with visitor context, live chat, popups, meeting booking and an AI call agent, plus a TAM Agent that builds audiences, maps buying committees and "Runs coordinated email + ads". <!-- src: https://www.warmly.ai/, checked 2026-11-02 -->
- **Albacross** acts through your CRM and outbound: it syncs hot accounts to Salesforce, HubSpot or Pipedrive, alerts reps in Slack or Teams, builds LinkedIn audiences, and starts AI-personalised email and LinkedIn sequences to decision-makers, from the Starter plan.

If you want a bot to talk to visitors in real time, choose Warmly. If you want hot accounts turned into sequences and CRM tasks without spreadsheets, Albacross does it for less.

## How to switch from Warmly to Albacross (or test both)

**Run Albacross next to Warmly for 14 days on the same traffic, compare the results on your target accounts, then decide before your Warmly renewal.** You don't need to remove Warmly to test. [[VERIFY: product to confirm the Albacross script runs alongside Warmly's without conflict]]

1. **Check your Warmly term.** Note whether you're on annual or quarterly billing and your renewal date. Quarterly billing gives you more exit points.
2. **Export what you want to keep** from Warmly: identified accounts, contacts already synced to your CRM, and any lists or audiences.
3. **Start an Albacross trial** at [/register](/register) (14 days, no credit card) and install the tracking script on your site.
4. **Agree a fair test.** Pick 15–30 target accounts and count how many each tool identifies over the same 14 days. Split results by region (US vs Europe), because that's where the two tools differ most.
5. **Connect your CRM and alerts.** Turn on Salesforce, HubSpot or Pipedrive sync and Slack or Teams alerts so reps see hot accounts where they already work.
6. **Rebuild outreach.** Set up AI email and LinkedIn sequences for the personas you used in Warmly, using Albacross's verified contact credits.
7. **Sort out GDPR paperwork** in parallel: sign the [DPA](/data-processing-agreement) and send your legal team's questions during the trial.
8. **Decide on the numbers:** identified target accounts, meetings booked and cost per year. If US person-level matches drove your pipeline, keep Warmly.
