## What each tool is

### Factors.ai in one paragraph
Factors.ai describes itself as "The AI ABM & Attribution Platform for GTM Teams" and says it is "Trusted by 1000+ GTM teams". <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/, checked 2026-11-02 --> It combines account identification, account scoring, multi-touch attribution, LinkedIn and Google ad tools ("Adpilot") and an AI assistant called Scout. Its centre of gravity is marketing analytics: which channels and campaigns move which accounts toward pipeline. It is operated by Slashbit Inc., a US-based company. <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/privacy-policy, checked 2026-11-02 -->

### Albacross in one paragraph
Albacross identifies the companies visiting your website, without forms or cookies on individual people. It scores them by fit and buying intent (on-site behaviour plus Bombora research signals) and pushes hot accounts to your CRM, Slack or Teams. It can also start AI-personalised email and LinkedIn outreach to the right decision-makers. Built in Stockholm for GDPR, with published pricing from €59/month.

## Key differences

### 1. Identification: company-level vs optional US person-level
**Both tools identify companies; only Factors adds person-level identification, and only for US traffic.** Factors says it identifies visitors "at both the account and individual contact levels (US traffic only)" and that "it can identify up to 40% of the US visitors via RB2B integration". <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/product/website-visitor-identification, checked 2026-11-02 --> On the pricing page, RB2B user identification is an add-on. <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/pricing, checked 2026-11-02 -->

Albacross is company-level only. If your pipeline depends on naming individual US visitors, Albacross is not the right fit, and Factors (or a person-level tool like RB2B) is. If most of your traffic is European, person-level identification is usually off the table anyway for legal reasons, and the question becomes which tool identifies more of *your* target companies. Neither vendor publishes an independent match-rate test. Factors claims "up to 75%"; we don't publish a single headline rate because it varies by traffic mix. [[VERIFY: publishable Albacross match-rate data by region]] The fair way to decide is to run both on the same site for the same 14 days and count matched target accounts.

### 2. Intent data
**Factors gives you more second-party intent (G2, LinkedIn); Albacross pairs website intent with Bombora research intent in an SMB-priced plan.** Factors lists G2 intent signals, LinkedIn intent signals and third-party intent upload, and sells Bombora intent topics as an add-on or a set number of topics (6 or 12) depending on plan. G2 impact reporting starts on Growth ($20K/year). <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/pricing, checked 2026-11-02 --> Albacross combines on-site behaviour (pages, repeat visits, hot pages) with off-site research intent from its [Bombora partnership](/newsroom/bombora-partnership) and scores every account by fit and intent in [Signal](/signal). [[VERIFY: Bombora intent plan inclusion]]

### 3. Attribution and analytics
**This is where Factors is clearly stronger.** Factors links "ad spend from LinkedIn, Google, Meta, and Bing to pipeline and revenue", supports view-through attribution for ads, tracks account-level journeys and reports conversion between funnel stages from MQL to closed-won. <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/product/analytics-and-attribution, checked 2026-11-02 --> Its pricing table also lists funnel, event, SEO, content and LLM-traffic analysis. <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/pricing, checked 2026-11-02 -->

Albacross is not a multi-touch attribution tool. It shows which source brought each identified company (useful to see whether a campaign reaches your ICP), but if your CMO needs a board-level attribution model, use Factors or a dedicated attribution platform. Smaller teams sometimes find this depth more than they need: one buyer we spoke to called a full attribution platform "over-engineered for their small team" (rep note, B2B team evaluating an attribution platform).

### 4. Outreach and activation
**Albacross has outreach built in; Factors hands contacts to other tools.** Albacross includes AI email sequences, AI LinkedIn sequences and verified email and phone credits from the Starter plan, so a marketer can go from "this company is on our pricing page" to a sequence to the right decision-maker without another subscription. [Automate](/automate) syncs hot accounts to your CRM, Slack, Teams and ad audiences.

Factors pushes data into "Apollo.io sequence" and "HeyReach for LinkedIn inmails" workflows, offers Slack and MS Teams alerts on every plan (2 automations on Lite, 10 on Basic, 15 on Growth), and sells "custom alerts and workflows on demand" and Scout agents as add-ons. <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/pricing, checked 2026-11-02 --> If you already run Apollo and HeyReach, that is a strength. If you don't, budget for them.

### 5. ABM advertising
**Factors goes deeper on paid media.** Its LinkedIn Adpilot covers audience sync, conversions API, impression control, view-through attribution, bulk exclusion and bid scheduling, with Google audience sync on Enterprise. <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/pricing, checked 2026-11-02 --> Albacross covers the essentials: LinkedIn Matched Audiences, a LinkedIn Ads integration on Professional and IP-based ABM display ads.

### 6. GDPR and where your data lives
**Both vendors say they comply with GDPR; the difference is where the data sits and how much of the product is built around EU rules.** Factors says it is "GDPR, CCPA, and PECR compliant" and "SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified". <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/pricing, checked 2026-11-02 --> Its security page says customer data is "stored and processed in GCP's 'us-west-1b' zone located in the United States" <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/security, checked 2026-11-02 -->, and its privacy policy relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses for transfers from the EEA. <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/privacy-policy, checked 2026-11-02 --> That is a lawful set-up, but it is a US transfer your legal team will need to review.

Albacross is company-level by design, built for GDPR and CCPA compliance, and offers a [Data Processing Agreement](/data-processing-agreement). For the legal background, see GDPR Article 6(1)(f) on legitimate interests ([gdpr-info.eu](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-6-gdpr/)) and the European Commission's page on the [EU-US Data Privacy Framework](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection/eu-us-data-transfers_en).

### 7. Integrations
**Both cover HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack and Teams; Albacross adds Pipedrive, Factors adds Zoho, Segment and more ad networks.** Factors lists HubSpot (companies + deals) and Salesforce (accounts + opportunities) sync, marked "full integration" on its higher plans, plus Zoho updates, LinkedIn, Google, Bing and Meta Ads, Google Search Console, G2, Segment and webhooks. <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/pricing, checked 2026-11-02 --> We found no Pipedrive integration on its integrations page. <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/integrations, checked 2026-11-02 --> Albacross has two-way Salesforce, HubSpot (Professional and up) and Pipedrive sync, Slack, Microsoft Teams, LinkedIn Matched Audiences, Google Sheets and an [IP-to-company API](/ip-to-company-api). ## Choose Factors.ai if…
- Multi-touch attribution and LinkedIn/Google ad orchestration are your main jobs, and identification is one input.
- Most of your traffic and pipeline is in the US and you want person-level identification of US visitors (RB2B add-on).
- You want G2 and LinkedIn intent in the same dashboard as your attribution.
- You already run Apollo.io or HeyReach for outreach.
- Your budget starts around $6K a year, and you're comfortable with annual contracts and US data hosting.

## Choose Albacross if…
- You sell in or into Europe and your legal team wants company-level identification from a European vendor with a DPA.
- You want identification, Bombora intent and outreach (AI email and LinkedIn sequences plus verified contacts) in one tool.
- You're an SMB or mid-market team that wants published pricing from €59/month and a 14-day trial with no credit card.
- You use Pipedrive, or want an IP-to-company API for personalisation.
- You don't need a full attribution model, or you already have one.

**Not the right fit if:** you need person-level identification of individual US visitors, or you only need basic company lists inside an existing CRM that already provides them.

**Can you use both?** Yes. Some teams keep an attribution platform for reporting and use a visitor identification tool for the sales side. If Factors is your attribution system of record, Albacross can still handle EU-focused identification and outreach. Check that you're not paying twice for the same company lists.

## How to switch from Factors.ai to Albacross
1. **Run both in parallel for 14 days.** Start an [Albacross trial](/register) (no credit card) while Factors is still live, so you compare the same traffic.
2. **Install the Albacross script** through Google Tag Manager or directly in your site header. Decide with your legal team whether it runs cookieless or after consent.
3. **Count matched target accounts.** Export your ABM or ICP list and compare how many each tool identified. Ignore headline match rates; count your own accounts.
4. **Connect your CRM and alerts.** Turn on Salesforce, HubSpot or Pipedrive sync and Slack or Teams alerts, and rebuild your key Factors segments as Albacross filters.
5. **Rebuild audiences and outreach.** Recreate LinkedIn Matched Audiences and set up AI email and LinkedIn sequences for your highest-intent segments.
6. **Export your Factors data before it's deleted.** Factors keeps data for 90 days after cancellation, then deletes it permanently. <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/pricing, checked 2026-11-02 --> Check your contract end date; Factors typically bills annually.
7. **Decide on attribution.** If you relied on Factors for attribution, keep it or move to an attribution platform; Albacross won't replace that part.

## FAQ

### Is Factors.ai worth it for a small B2B team?
It depends on whether you need attribution. Factors Lite costs $199/month, but CRM sync, LinkedIn influence reporting and longer data retention start on Basic at $6K/year. <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/pricing, checked 2026-11-02 --> If a small team mainly needs to see which companies visit and reach out to them, a company-level tool with built-in outreach, such as Albacross from €59/month billed yearly, usually costs less.

### How much does Factors.ai cost?
Factors.ai lists four plans: Lite at $199/month after trial, Basic at $6K/year, Growth at $20K/year and Enterprise from $36K/year. Add-ons such as Scout credits, RB2B user identification and Bombora topics cost extra. <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/pricing, checked 2026-11-02 -->

### Does Factors.ai identify individual website visitors?
Only in the US, through an add-on. Factors identifies companies globally and says it can identify "up to 40% of the US visitors" at person level via its RB2B integration. <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/product/website-visitor-identification, checked 2026-11-02 --> Albacross is company-level only, everywhere.

### Is Factors.ai GDPR-compliant, and where is its data stored?
Factors says it is GDPR, CCPA and PECR compliant and SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified. <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/pricing, checked 2026-11-02 --> It stores and processes customer data in Google Cloud in the United States and uses the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses for EU data. <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/security, checked 2026-11-02 --> <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/privacy-policy, checked 2026-11-02 --> Ask your DPO whether a US transfer is acceptable for your use case.

### Does Albacross do multi-touch attribution like Factors?
No. Albacross shows which source brought each identified company, but it doesn't build multi-touch attribution models. If attribution is your main need, Factors or a dedicated attribution platform is the better fit.

### Which is cheaper, Albacross or Factors.ai?
Albacross has the lower entry price: Starter from €59/month billed yearly, against $199/month for Factors Lite. Factors Lite does include more identified companies per month (up to 1,500). Compare total cost including outreach: Albacross includes AI sequences and contact credits, while Factors outreach runs through Apollo.io or HeyReach.

### Is there a free trial for Albacross and Factors.ai?
Yes, both. Albacross offers 14 days free with no credit card. Factors offers a free trial on its Lite plan with no credit card required; the page doesn't state the length. <!-- src: https://www.factors.ai/pricing, checked 2026-11-02 -->

### What are the best Factors.ai alternatives?
It depends on what you used Factors for. For EU-focused company identification with outreach, Albacross. For attribution, Dreamdata or HockeyStack. For US person-level identification, RB2B or Warmly. For enterprise ABM, 6sense or Demandbase. See the use-case table above.
