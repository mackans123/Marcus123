## What is website visitor identification software (and how does it work)?

Website visitor identification software tells you which organisations (and, in some tools, which people) visit your website, even when they never fill in a form. It turns anonymous sessions into named accounts you can score, route to sales and engage.

The first question we hear from buyers is "which companies are actually on our site, and are they relevant?" One CMO in UK manufacturing wanted "strong filtering tools to help us separate the noise from the genuine leads."

There are two technical approaches, and the difference matters more than any feature list.

### Company-level identification (reverse IP to company)

A small script records each visit's IP address. The tool matches it against a database of IP ranges used by businesses, names the company and adds firmographics such as size, industry, revenue, location and technology. Better tools add on-site behaviour and off-site intent data to score buying readiness.

It works globally because it doesn't need to know who the person is. Its limits: home-office and mobile traffic often resolves to an internet service provider (ISP) instead of an employer, and VPNs hide the real network. One analytics consultancy told us "a significant portion of the identified visitors are showing up as Internet Service Providers… our monthly identification quota is being used on companies that are not useful to us."

### Person-level identification (identity graphs)

Person-level tools try to name the individual. They match the visitor's browser to an identity graph built from cookies, hashed emails and partner data, and return a name, LinkedIn profile or business email. Because this relies on consumer data, most person-level tools restrict it to US traffic. RB2B's pricing page states that "Contact-level (person) ID is US only." <!-- src: https://www.rb2b.com/pricing, checked 2026-11-02 --> Apollo says its contact-level identification is "currently available for US-based visitors." <!-- src: https://www.apollo.io/product/website-visitor-identification, checked 2026-11-02 -->

### Visitor tracking vs visitor identification

Website visitor *tracking* software, such as Google Analytics 4, counts sessions, sources and page views but doesn't say who the visitor is. Website visitor *identification* software names the company (or person) behind the session. Most B2B teams run both.

## Company-level vs person-level: which do you need?

Choose company-level identification if you sell to accounts, have meaningful European or UK traffic, or need an answer your legal team will accept. Choose person-level identification if most of your pipeline is in the US and your SDRs want named people to call today.

| Question | Company-level fits better | Person-level fits better |
|---|---|---|
| Where is your traffic? | EU, UK, Nordics, DACH, global | Mostly US |
| Legal risk appetite | Low: legal reviews tracking scripts | Higher: US-only consumer-data graphs accepted |
| Sales motion | Account-based: buying committees, ABM | 1:1 SDR follow-up on named individuals |
| What you do next | Find decision-makers, score by intent, run ABM ads | Email or call the specific visitor |
| Typical tools | Albacross, Leadfeeder, Snitcher, Leadinfo, Lead Forensics, SalesViewer | RB2B, Warmly, Instantly, Happierleads |

Albacross identifies companies, then helps you reach the right decision-makers there with verified contact credits. If you need the named individual who visited, a person-level tool such as RB2B or Warmly fits better.

## Why B2B teams buy (or switch) visitor identification tools

These are the reasons buyers gave us in sales calls, trials and support conversations between May and November 2026 (anonymised).

1. **"Analytics shows visits, not companies."** Teams want to see who is researching them without waiting for a form fill.
2. **Noise eats the quota.** One UK sales agency asked for a tool that will "filter out ISPs, mobile carriers, VPN/hosting traffic and bots so our reps aren't chasing noise."
3. **Manual work.** CSV exports, spreadsheet copying, tagging hundreds of pages by hand and manual lead hand-offs.
4. **Unclear limits and pricing.** "Did we hit the limit, or not?" is a real support question, as is "is it billed per identified company per month?"
5. **Cookie consent and legal questions.** Several EU buyers found their tool only fired after cookie acceptance, which lowered volume.
6. **"Will it pay off at our scale?"** Small teams with low traffic ask for an honest assessment and a small pilot before committing.
7. **"Our CRM already does it."** Some HubSpot users decide the native feature is enough; see Breeze Intelligence below.

## Is website visitor identification legal under GDPR?

Company-level website visitor identification can be run lawfully under GDPR in most set-ups, because it identifies organisations rather than people. But the tracking script, any personal data processed along the way (an IP address can be personal data) and any follow-up outreach each need a legal basis. Person-level identification of EU visitors is much harder to justify, which is why most person-level tools limit it to US traffic. [[VERIFY: Legal: approve this whole section and the GDPR FAQ answer before publishing.]]

What to check, in order:

1. **The legal basis.** Most company-level vendors rely on legitimate interest under [Article 6(1)(f) GDPR](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-6-gdpr/): processing "necessary for the purposes of the legitimate interests pursued by the controller or by a third party," unless overridden by the individual's rights. Leadinfo and Snitcher both state this basis publicly. <!-- src: https://www.leadinfo.com/en/pricing/, checked 2026-11-02 --> <!-- src: https://www.snitcher.com/pricing, checked 2026-11-02 --> You still need a documented legitimate interest assessment.
2. **The script and cookie consent.** Storing or reading information on a visitor's device falls under [Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2002/58/oj). The European Data Protection Board's [Guidelines 2/2023 on the technical scope of Art. 5(3)](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/guidelines/guidelines-22023-technical-scope-art-53-eprivacy-directive_en) (final version dated 2024-10-16) explain how broadly that applies. <!-- src: edpb.europa.eu, checked 2026-11-02 --> Decide with your legal team whether your script runs before or after consent, and ask vendors which set-ups they support.
3. **A Data Processing Agreement.** Your vendor should sign a DPA. Albacross's is at [/data-processing-agreement](/data-processing-agreement); see also [Albacross and GDPR](/newsroom/albacross-gdpr).
4. **Data hosting and sub-processors.** Ask every vendor where data is stored and who processes it.
5. **Outreach.** Contacting decision-makers is a separate processing activity with its own rules, including national e-marketing laws.

US person-level tools such as RB2B and Apollo exclude EU visitors because naming an individual from a consumer identity graph needs a lawful basis that is hard to establish for EU residents.

## How to choose the right tool for your team

Start with where your buyers are, then how you'll act on the data.

## How to test match rates before you buy

Every vendor publishes a different "identification rate": Leadinfo says around 30% of traffic, RB2B claims 35–45% US coverage on Pro+, Factors says over 75% of companies. They measure different things on different traffic. The only fair comparison is on your own site.

1. **Run two (or three) trials at the same time** for 14 days on the same website.
2. **Install every script the same way** (same tag manager, same consent behaviour).
3. **Count unique companies identified,** not sessions or "visits".
4. **Remove noise:** ISPs, mobile carriers, hosting/VPN networks, bots and your own company.
5. **Measure ICP fit:** how many remaining companies match your target accounts or ideal customer profile? This matters more than raw volume.
6. **Spot-check false positives:** check whether 20 identified companies' visit patterns make sense.
7. **Split by region:** compare EU, UK and US traffic separately; tools differ most here.
8. **Test the workflow:** time how long an identified account takes to reach your CRM, Slack or Teams, and a sequence.

## FAQ

### What is the best website visitor identification software?

It depends on your market. For European or GDPR-sensitive B2B teams that want company-level identification, intent scoring and outreach in one tool with a published price, Albacross is the best fit. For person-level identification of US visitors, choose RB2B or Warmly. To start free, use Leadfeeder Lite or RB2B's free plan.

### Can I see exactly who visits my website?

You can usually see which company visited, not which person. Company-level tools match the visitor's IP address to a business. Person-level tools name individuals mainly for US traffic, using identity graphs, and most exclude EU visitors. For EU traffic, the practical route is to identify the company and then find the right decision-makers.

### Is website visitor identification GDPR compliant?

Company-level identification can be GDPR compliant when it has a documented legal basis (usually legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f)), a lawful script and consent set-up, and a signed DPA with the vendor. Person-level identification of EU residents is much harder to justify. Ask each vendor for its legal basis, DPA and hosting location in writing.

### How accurate is website visitor identification?

Accuracy varies by tool, region and traffic mix, and published figures are vendor claims. Examples checked on 2026-11-02: Leadinfo says around 30% of traffic, RB2B claims 35–45% US person-level coverage on Pro+, and Omron identified 28% of visitors with Albacross versus 4% with its previous tool. Remote workers, VPNs and mobile networks lower match rates for every IP-based tool, so run a side-by-side trial on your own traffic.

Yes, with limits. ### Does visitor identification work with remote workers and VPNs?

Partly. Company-level tools rely on business IP addresses, so visitors on home broadband, mobile networks or VPNs often resolve to an ISP rather than their employer. Good tools let you filter that noise, but no IP-based tool identifies every remote visitor.

### Can I automatically contact companies that visit my website?

Yes. Albacross Engage can start AI-personalised email and LinkedIn sequences to decision-makers at high-intent visiting companies, using verified contact credits, from the Starter plan. Warmly, Apollo and Instantly offer similar follow-up for US contacts. In the EU, B2B outreach has its own legal rules, separate from identification, so check national e-marketing laws.
